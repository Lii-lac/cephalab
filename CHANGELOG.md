# Changelog

## v0.2.3 (2026-11-26)

- When you're done playing, the Discord card goes away. With a CFW Switch, it clears as soon as you close Splatoon 3.
- Without CFW (or with the Switch asleep), the card shows only while SplatNet has a recent match: about 5 minutes after your last battle, or 10 after your last Salmon Run shift started. Your first game of a session shows up once it ends.

## v0.2.2 (2026-11-26)

- After you restart Splatoon 3, Cephalab finds your outfit again by itself once the game settles, so your weapon comes back on the Discord card without opening Cephalab.

## v0.2.1 (2026-11-26)

- Discord Rich Presence works on Mac, the same card as on Windows.
- One-click updates on Mac: Cephalab downloads the new version, checks it, and swaps itself in, keeping your old copy until the new one is in place.
- Mac players install this one by hand (unzip, drag into Applications, **Open Anyway** the first time). Every update after it is one click.

## v0.2.0 (2026-11-26)

- Discord Rich Presence. With a CFW Switch it's live: where you are (lobby, Splatsville, Inkopolis, Alterna, Grizzco, the shops, Side Order), the stage and mode of your match, the match type, your party size, your Anarchy rank, and Salmon Run with your Salmometer.
- Your weapon sits on the card in the color of the mode you're playing.
- Without CFW, the card shows your last match from SplatNet.
- Pick how much it shares under **Settings**: Off, Basic or Full.
- First macOS build, for Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). Not notarized, so the first launch needs **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
- Finished gear gets a "done" tape on Path.
- A new 3D squid icon in the browser tab.
- Logins are kept in the macOS Keychain on Mac and Windows Credential Manager on Windows.

## v0.1.1 (2026-11-24)

- Cephalab sits in the system tray, with its own squid icon.
- Start the companion from the offline notice with one click.
- A log you can copy from **Settings → Data** for bug reports, with login tokens scrubbed out.
- The first drink search runs right away instead of five minutes after boot.
- An **Open an issue** button in the README.

## v0.1.0 (2026-11-24)

First beta.

- Weapons & Gear: browse top community builds from sendou.ink or save your own.
- Build Editor: live ability stats as you swap weapons, gear, mains and subs.
- Path Planner: plans all three gear pieces together (drinks, rolls, scrubs and chunks).
- Live sync with a Nintendo account (no CFW required) or a modded Switch through sys-botbase.
- Automatic or manual update checks under **Settings → Data**.
